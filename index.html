what it is

FYP is the first piece in a new body of work by sirius crocodile, drawn frame by frame, scanned in on blush paper. the camera looks back. you scroll anyway. that’s the piece.

it lives on tezos, mints through objkt, and is included in the official objkt one curation — publicly exhibited at nfc lisbon this june.
