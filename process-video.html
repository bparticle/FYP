Voiceover transcript

I was extremely happy to receive an invitation from Objkt to take part in the #FYP exhibition on Tezos. So far I had only been collecting art on the blockchain, but I had never minted a piece. This event would be my genesis drop, so the stakes were high.

#FYP is short for For You Page, the algorithmic sorting and recommendation system popularized by TikTok and adopted by most social platforms. We were asked to reflect on that concept from the artist’s point of view.

I was excited about the project and jumped straight into it with some sketches and ideas. Some of them ended up on the cutting floor. This was one of those projects that came about very spontaneously. I made a few sketches, I created a few animations, but only when I stepped back and looked at what I had made did the full picture start to emerge.

I started with a person swiping down, liking everything while crying, and to me that picture captures the disconnect that often occurs between the real flesh-and-blood person and the online persona we can manipulate and control in many different ways.

The second piece I started with was a highly anxious person absorbing the timeline as his soul is apparently leaving through a huge hole in the back of his head. These two pieces were the prelude to the final and most elaborate animation I created for the exhibition.

It’s two figures with the same escaping souls, ceaselessly hitting the algorithm, consuming flashes of content while being watched by a camera in the corner.

Since now I had three pieces, naturally it turned out to be a triptych. And the classical, religious connotation of this format made perfect sense to me once I stepped back from it.

People have accused me of making the dopest physicals in the past and it never stopped me from researching and improving my craft. This time though, I think I really made something special with the triptych, because I created an actual folding, palm-size artifact that you can hold or put on your desk, and I think it’s my best work yet.

I’m awarding this physical to the collector who manages to pick up all three pieces. Supplicant is the 1/1, Altar is an open edition, and Possession is a limited edition of 25 pieces. Everything drops on Friday at 2pm in some time zone, but you can already place offers on the limited edition ahead of the opening.

I hope you like the work. Good luck!
